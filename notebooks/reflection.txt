My first approach was to let the AI generate the entire project at once, before loading any data. The code assumed column names (region, naive) from an old blog post that didn't exist in the real dataset, and the forecast tested on 2026 data that had silently collapsed to near-zero — producing a fake MAE of 1.26. I switched to a step-by-step process: load real data first, verify every assumption, run each cell myself. I clipped the series at 2025-08-31 (data artifact) and fixed the test window. The final version is better because its results are real and defensible: best baseline MAE 14.28 (~31% of the mean), with an honest conclusion that the series resists prediction.
